Good to know

A few quick
answers.

Questions about how ShrinkDrop works? Start here.

Will ShrinkDrop change my original photos?+

No. It writes compressed copies to your chosen destination and leaves the source files untouched.

Which image formats can I use?+

You can add JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, TIFF, GIF, and HEIC/HEIF images. Choose JPEG, WebP, or AVIF for your compressed output.

Does ShrinkDrop compress video?+

Not yet. The first version focuses on image batches; video compression is not included.

Do my images get uploaded?+

No. Image processing happens locally on your Mac. ShrinkDrop does not need to upload your files to a server.

Is ShrinkDrop a subscription?+

No. ShrinkDrop is a one-time payment: CA$5 or US$5 based on the checkout currency. Dodo emails an activation key after purchase; enter it in the app to unlock compression.

Can I try it before buying?+

Selected beta testers receive a free 24-hour trial. Join the waitlist and we’ll email the download and next steps if you’re selected.
